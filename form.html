Request your $100 AWS credits

If your team is taking part in the hackathon, you can claim $100 in AWS credits to use on your project. Just fill out the form below with your team and project details and we'll get the credits over to you. Only one person per team needs to submit this, so check with your teammates before filling it out to avoid duplicates.

You'll get the code in your email. To claim the credits you'll need to add a card or UPI to your AWS account, but don't worry, you won't be charged for anything up to $100, which is plenty to build your project with. You can also set spending limits in your account if you want, and if you run into any issues just reach out to contact@wemakedevs.org.

You don't need to wait for the credits to get started. You can already work on ideation, run your projects locally, build out the actual project, and use the open-source AWS tools we've mentioned on the website. Once your credits come through, you can deploy it all on AWS.

We'll start sending out the codes today, so you can begin using them tomorrow and over the weekend.
